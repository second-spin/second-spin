Safety and trust

Pass it on with care.

A free item is only a good gift if it is suitable for the next skater. Please describe wear and defects honestly.

01

Inspect before use

Check boots, frames, plates, axles, wheels, bearings and fasteners. Cracked or structurally damaged equipment should not be passed on as ready to use.

02

Protective gear needs care

Helmets should fit correctly and have no known impact damage. Do not donate cracked helmets or compromised pads. When history is unknown, disclose it and seek qualified advice.

03

Adults arrange handovers

Use a skating club, coach or public location where possible. Never ask children to meet donors alone.

04

Keep personal details private

Do not publish phone numbers, home addresses, children's identifying details or photos without appropriate consent. Share contact details only when needed and with permission.
