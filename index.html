WHY SECOND SPIN

Small handovers.
Big possibilities.

Skating gear can be expensive, and children grow fast. Meanwhile, wheels, boots and protective equipment often sit unused after a season or competition.

Second Spin brings the community together so usable gear can move from one skater to the next — thoughtfully, safely and for free.

CARE COMES FIRST

Pass it on
with care.

Free gear should still be fit for purpose. Honest descriptions and sensible checks help everyone.

01

Check condition

Do not describe cracked, structurally damaged or faulty equipment as ready to use. Disclose wear and defects.

02

Protective gear matters

Do not pass on cracked helmets or compromised pads. Unknown impact history should be disclosed and assessed carefully.

03

Adults coordinate handovers

Use a club, coach or public location where possible. Children should not meet donors alone.

04

Respect privacy

Do not publish children's identifying details, home addresses or private contact information.
